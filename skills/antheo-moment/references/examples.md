# Examples: what goes wrong, and the fix

Each pair is shown live, before and after, on the Antheo skills page. Without the skill, then with it:

| Without | With |
|---|---|
| A spinner and “Processing…”: you can’t tell what’s happening | Each real step in words: checking the card, sending the money |
| “Done” swaps in: it worked and nobody noticed | A tick that draws itself and the colour turns: paid |
| The same effect on every button in the app | Saved for the rare moment that matters, like a payment |
