# Questions, by lens

Open this when a lens in `SKILL.md` finds something and the one-line question there is too blunt to say what. Each
question has a visible answer on the screen. Ask only the ones the screen raises, and keep the cap of seven points.
A number below is a starting estimate for a screenshot, never a measurement: say "looks about".

## Job

- Cover each block in turn. Does the person lose anything they came for?
- What is the one action? Is it the most visible thing that can be pressed?
- What is shown that is empty, finished or not relevant yet?
- Where would a first-time visitor look first, and is that the right place?

## Direction

- Could this screen belong to any product in any field if the logo were swapped?
- Which choice here was made for this audience, and which arrived by default?
- Is there one idea, or several effects sharing a page?

## Type

- Squint: how many levels of text can you count? More than three or four?
- Is the largest text the most important thing, or only the first thing?
- Is anything set in grey that a person has to read to finish the job?
- Do lines run past about 75 characters, or break after one word?

## Colour

- Remove the colour: does the screen still say what is on, chosen and wrong?
- How many things are coloured, and do they all mean the same kind of thing?
- Does body text look at least as dark as a mid grey on white (about 4.5:1), and do icons and edges that carry
  meaning stand out from what they sit on (about 3:1)?
- In dark, is the page the darkest thing and each layer above it lighter?

## Layout

- Draw a line down the left edge of the content. What steps off it, and why?
- Are things that belong together closer to each other than to anything else?
- Do matching siblings (cards, rows, tiles) match in width, padding and the order inside?
- At phone width, what is the first screen, and does it hold the job?

## Depth

- How many kinds of edge are there (line, shadow, fill)? Would one do?
- Does anything float that has no reason to (a card inside a card, a shadow on a flat list)?
- Do fields look like places to type before anyone clicks them?

## Quiet

- Count text sizes, weights, corner sizes, outlines, tags and dividers. Which would nobody miss?
- Is there a tag, an icon or a helper line on everything, so none of them stands out?

## Controls

- Does each control look like what it does, at rest?
- Is there exactly one filled button in view?
- What happens after the press: is loading, success and failure drawn, or only the resting state?
- Are choices shown to pick from, or does the person have to remember and type?

## Numbers

- Can two figures in a column be compared at a glance (aligned, same decimals, same unit)?
- Does every figure say what it counts and over what period?
- Is a change coloured by good and bad, or only by up and down?

## Words

- Read only the buttons. Do they say what happens?
- Is there a sentence that could be on any product's page?
- Does the error say what to do next?

## Motion

- If the movement were removed, would the person lose track of what changed?
- Does anything move that is used many times a day?

## Access

- Where does Tab go first, and can you see it?
- Is anything said by colour, position or hover alone?
- At 200% zoom, what is cut off?

## Touch

- Could a thumb hit each control without hitting its neighbour (about 44px)?
- Is anything only reachable by hover?
- What does the keyboard cover when a field is focused?
