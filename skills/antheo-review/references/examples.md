# Examples: what goes wrong, and the fix

Each pair is shown live, before and after, on the Antheo skills page. Without the skill, then with it:

| Without | With |
|---|---|
| An opinion: “Looks really clean!” | A verdict first: ready or not, and how it was checked |
| “Consider accessibility”, with no place and no fix | Each finding with where it is and the cheapest fix |
| Silent about what it didn’t look at | Says what wasn’t checked, so nothing unseen is approved |
