# Examples: what goes wrong, and the fix

Each pair is shown live, before and after, on the Antheo skills page. Without the skill, then with it:

| Without | With |
|---|---|
| A sparkle badge above the headline | Nothing above the headline; the headline says what it is |
| One word in a purple gradient | One colour for the whole headline, and a word that is specific |
| Two buttons that compete: “Get Started”, “Learn More” | One button that says what you get |
| Three icon boxes that could be on any site | The real product: an invoice, with real numbers |

## From the lab

Real builds by coding agents with these skills, judged by people beside the reference:

| What went wrong | What fixed it |
|---|---|
| Builds without the skills copied the reference's own words and character ("BUSY DOING NOTHIN'", the daisy) (round 4) | Match the reference's look and effects; write your own words and draw your own character |

## When the default is wrong

An expressive component built from a reference (a sticker headline, a widget) takes a direction even inside an app; the page around it stays calm.
