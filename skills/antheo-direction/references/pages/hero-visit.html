What happens at a fitting

  1. We measure both feet

    Standing, for length and for width. Most children have one foot a little longer than the other.

  2. Your child tries two or three pairs

    Only pairs that fit. They walk the length of the shop and back, and we check the heel and the toes.

  3. You decide, with no rush

    If nothing fits well today, we say so, and note the size so we can tell you when the right pair arrives.

Prices

The fitting is free. School shoes are £45 to £62, trainers £38 to £55, wellies £24.

A free re-measure any time in the six months after you buy.
