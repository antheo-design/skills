# Ideas: plays by component

Starting points, one line each: the real thing it is like, and what that thing does that feels good. Pick one idea
per component and carry it through every layer; never stack several.

## Controls

| Component | Like | The play |
|---|---|---|
| Toggle | a light switch | resists, then snaps past the middle; the light it controls warms on |
| Toggle (soft) | a jelly button | squashes on press, wobbles back, fills with colour when on |
| Checkbox | a tick drawn in ink | the stroke draws in the hand's direction; a tiny ink splash when it finishes |
| Slider | a mixing-desk fader | a notch at zero and at the default that the thumb sinks into; the track fills behind it |
| Stepper (+ and −) | a mechanical counter | the number rolls; holding repeats faster and faster; the ends bump |
| Picker, dial | a radio or safe dial | it clicks at each option, keeps a little momentum, settles on the nearest |
| Segmented control | a sliding switch plate | the plate slides with a spring and squashes slightly as it stops |
| Rating | a row of lights | each lights in turn up to the choice; the last one glints |
| Colour picker | a paint drop | the swatch wobbles like a drop when chosen; the drop merges into the preview |

## Buttons and actions

| Component | Like | The play |
|---|---|---|
| Primary button | a key | presses in, springs back; while working, the label rolls through the real steps |
| Like or save | a heart, a bookmark | pops with a short spring; a few particles on the first like only |
| Copy | a stamp | the icon stamps down; the label rolls to "Copied" and back |
| Delete | a paper shredder | the row slides out in strips, with Undo for a few seconds |
| Send | a paper plane | the icon lifts and flies off, a new one slides in |

## Containers and lists

| Component | Like | The play |
|---|---|---|
| Card | a physical card | tilts towards the pointer with a glare; lifts on press |
| Card stack, wallet | a fanned hand of cards | fans on hover, flicks to the next with momentum |
| List reorder | tiles on a table | the picked tile lifts with a shadow; others slide aside on a spring |
| Tabs | a sliding marker | the marker stretches towards the new tab, then snaps its tail in |
| Accordion | a drawer | slides out with a small settle, contents follow a beat later |

## Feedback

| Component | Like | The play |
|---|---|---|
| Progress | a kettle, a glass filling | the level rises with a wave on top; it steams near the end |
| Loading | a record, a spinning top | the thing it loads spins or wobbles, never a generic spinner |
| Empty state | a quiet desk, a sleeping pet | a character dozes; it wakes and points when the person hovers the action |
| Error | a puzzled character | it shrugs, names the fix, perks up when retried |
| Success | a stamp, a bell | one finish beat (antheo-moment): stamp lands, bell rings, then still |

## Widgets and objects

| Component | Like | The play |
|---|---|---|
| Radio | a real radio | static and hiss between stations, a needle that wobbles and settles, a character that bops |
| Weather | a window | the sky moves, rain runs down the glass, the light follows the hour |
| Music player | a cassette or record | the reels or the record turn with play, slow to a stop on pause |
| Timer | a kitchen timer | wind it by dragging round; it ticks down and rings |
| Thermostat | a dial on the wall | the ring warms in colour as it turns, clicks at each degree |
| Headline, sticker | a sticker on a laptop | peels at the corner on hover, wobbles when pressed back down |
