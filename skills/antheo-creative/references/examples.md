# Examples: what goes wrong, and the fix

## From the lab

Real builds by coding agents with these skills, judged by people beside the reference:

| What went wrong | What fixed it |
|---|---|
| A radio widget that jumped from station to station: correct, and nobody played with it (round 4, with skills) | The baseline's radio filled its screen with static while tuning and cleared as a station came in; people kept tuning it. Find the toy and make it the richest part |
| The fun trimmed to fit "under 300ms" and "no grain" rules meant for app chrome (round 4) | The play keeps its own timing and the object's own feedback; only the always-list binds it |
| An extruded headline whose 3D swayed a little, timidly (round 3) | The build people chose swung its depth as far as the reference did and drew the edges of every side plane |
| Candy buttons that squashed nicely with three options and broke with two and five (round 4) | Design the play for every count and state, then look at each |

## When the default is wrong

A dense data table or a form filled under pressure: keep play to a press that feels physical and at most one small surprise.
