# Antheo Motion

Motion is information about change: something arrived, left, moved or answered you. Motion that explains nothing is
noise; a change with no motion is a jump. This skill decides which changes move and gives each its exact timing. A
component's one signature moment belongs to **antheo-moment**; the duration tokens are generated by
**antheo-tokens**; the reduced-motion requirement is checked by **antheo-access**. Auditing, cutting or planning the motion across a
whole app is **antheo-motion-audit**'s.

## Decide in this order

1. **List every change** the screen makes: what opens, closes, appears, leaves, reorders, updates, loads, succeeds
   or fails.
2. **Mark how often** a person sees each: many times a day, daily, or rarely.
3. **Give each its duration and easing** from the tables below.
4. **Hand the one rare, meaningful event**, if there is one, to antheo-moment.
5. **Check it** fast, twice, and with reduced motion, then **film it** and read the frames (below).

## A still reference: design the motion it implies

A screenshot or a still design shows only the resting state. Before building, write down how the real thing moves:
what each press does, how each value changes, what it does while nothing happens. Then give every control its press
answer, every state change its transition, and the main action one bigger beat, all with the timing below. Matching
the still exactly and leaving it motionless is not matching it.

## Name why it moves, or don't move it

"This moves so the person understands ___." Can't finish the sentence: no animation.

## The more often it is seen, the less it moves

| How often | It gets |
|---|---|
| Many times a day (typing, rows, tabs, menus, a palette) | at most 150ms, 4px of travel; from the keyboard, nothing but a short fade |
| Daily (a dialog, a sheet, a toast, a save) | a short arrival and a quicker exit, 150 to 300ms |
| Rarely (a first success, an order placed) | the one place for a signature moment (antheo-moment) |

## The durations, by job

| Token | Value | Takes |
|---|---|---|
| `--dur-instant` | 100ms | colour and opacity feedback |
| `--dur-fast` | 150ms | press, hover in and out, a menu or tooltip opening |
| `--dur-base` | 200ms | things leaving, a dialog closing, a value or text swap, a toast arriving |
| `--dur-open` | 250ms | a dialog opening, tabs, an accordion, a card resizing |
| `--dur-slow` | 300ms | a sheet or drawer rising, a marker sliding across |
| `--dur-page` | 400ms | a page or shared-element transition |

Interface motion stays at or under 300ms; page moves at or under 400ms. Spinners, progress and scroll-linked motion
run `linear`.

## Open and close, as pairs

| Thing | Opens | Closes |
|---|---|---|
| Menu, popover, select | 150ms, from `scale(0.97)` and a fade, at the trigger | 120ms |
| Tooltip | 400ms delay, then a 150ms fade; at once within 300ms of another closing | at once |
| Dialog | 250ms: 8px rise, fade, scale from 0.96 | 200ms |
| Sheet, drawer | 300ms from its edge, fully opaque throughout (no fade, no blur) | 200ms |
| Toast | 200ms, 12px rise | 200ms, out the way it came |
| Accordion, height reveal | 250ms, `grid-template-rows: 0fr` to `1fr` | 200ms |

Never delay a close or a hover-out: shorten the duration instead.

## One curve for arriving and leaving

`--ease-out` `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)` for everything that enters or leaves. `--ease-in-out`
`cubic-bezier(0.77, 0, 0.175, 1)` only for something moving from one place on screen to another. `--ease-in` only
for something thrown away for good. Exits are quicker than entrances and travel about half as far.

## Small distances, never from zero

Travel 4px for a value swap or a popover, 8px for a dialog, 12px for a toast, 24px for a hero part; the full edge only
for a sheet. Enter from `scale(0.96)` (`0.97` for menus, `0.98` for tooltips) with opacity 0, never from `scale(0)`.
Blur 4px on a text or icon swap, 8px on a large surface, 2px on a crossfade under 200ms.

## What may animate

`transform` and `opacity` (and colour) by default; a short `filter` blur, `clip-path`, and `grid-template-rows` for
height reveals where needed. Never `transition: all`, never `width`, `height`, `top`, `left` or `margin`, never
`box-shadow` itself: put a changing shadow on a pseudo-element and fade it. `will-change` only after a profile asks.

## Grow from the trigger

Menus and popovers take their `transform-origin` at the trigger (Radix exposes
`--radix-popover-content-transform-origin`); a modal stays centred; a sheet comes from the edge it lives on; a
shared element (a card opening into its page) uses one `view-transition-name` on each side.

## Transitions for state, keyframes for one-shot

Anything a person can toggle uses a CSS transition or a spring, which reverse mid-flight; keyframes only for things
that play once.

## Stagger 30 to 40ms, the first eight, 300ms in all

60 to 80ms for a hero's three to six parts. Never stagger something opened often, and never block a click while items
arrive.

## Springs only where a hand moves it

A switch knob, a released drag, a gliding segmented pill. Bounce 0 to 0.2 on controls (the overshoot stays inside the
track), up to 0.45 for a rare celebration; one wobble at most. A drag follows the finger with no transition and, on
release, springs from the release speed; a sheet closes on a flick faster than about 0.11px per
ms, or once it has travelled `min(64px, 15%)` of its height, otherwise it springs back (`references/gestures.md`).

## Nothing jumps

Every value that changes is seen to change; a removed row closes its gap; space is reserved for what appears (layout
shift under 0.1). On load, at most one entrance. Switch themes with transitions off for one frame.

## Reduced motion keeps the story in fades

Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, travel, scale, blur, parallax and bounces become a 150 to 200ms opacity
crossfade; a switch knob moves at once; autoplaying loops stop. Anything that moves for more than 5 seconds by itself
has a pause control (WCAG 2.2.2); nothing flashes more than 3 times a second (2.3.1).

## Across a whole app

1. **Find:** a table of every change, how often it is seen, how it moves now, how it should.
2. **Improve:** tokens for every number, the missing exits, origin at the trigger, one pattern at a time.
3. **Remove:** anything that animates on every load, plays on a many-times-a-day action, delays the next action,
   loops while people read, or runs over 300ms on a routine action. When unsure, remove.
4. **Approve or block:** a reason sentence, tokens only, the allowed properties only, an exit quicker than the enter,
   reduced motion handled, watched at normal speed and at 4× CPU slowdown.

## Check it

Click everything fast, twice: it reverses, never jumps. Open the menu twenty times: still quick. Throttle the CPU
4×: still smooth. Turn on reduced motion: nothing travels.

## Watch it: a still can't judge motion

A screenshot of the resting state says nothing about how a thing moves, and every checker reads still frames. So
film each motion you made and look at the frames before calling it done:

```
node <antheo-motion>/scripts/filmstrip.mjs <url> --clip ".tuner" --click ".preset" --duration 600
node <antheo-motion>/scripts/filmstrip.mjs <url> --clip ".sheet" --drag ".handle" --by 0,240 --duration 900
```

It slows the page to 0.1× (CSS, Web Animations and the JavaScript clock), does the one thing, and lays 12 frames of
the element on one sheet, each with its time. Open the sheet and read every frame:

- each frame looks composed on its own: no hard edge sliding through, no flash of the wrong colour, no empty box
- every value that changes is seen to change: a number that should roll doesn't snap while its needle still travels
- nothing reflows or jumps mid-way; the first frame already answers the press
- the end settles where it should: a spring overshoots a little and comes back, a control doesn't stop dead
- film it again with `--reduce` and `--dark`: the story is still told, and dark has no flash of light

Fix what a frame shows, film it again, and keep the last sheet as the evidence.

## What generated UI gets wrong here

Check each in `references/habits.md` before calling it done: transition: all · content hidden until a script reveals
it · a blanket reduced-motion reset · a focus ring that fades or slides in · motion that performs · hover effects on
things that aren't clickable · a transition reading a name nobody defined.

## When more motion is right

The purpose and frequency rules decide; the numbers are defaults. A game, a creative tool, a learning product or a
brand moment the person asked for may move more and longer. It still answers input at once, never blocks the next
action, can be interrupted mid-way, respects reduced motion, and shows its cost: a slow frame on a mid-range phone
is a finding whatever the style.
