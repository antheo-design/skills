# What generated dashboards get wrong

Part of antheo-data. Read it before calling the work done, and check the build against each habit.

Seen again and again in agent-built dashboards (2025 to 26 write-ups and tool comparisons). Check for each.

- **A sparkline beside every figure,** with no axis, label or period. Remove it, or draw a real chart.
- **A pie for a ranking or many categories,** a 3D chart, two unrelated axes, twenty lines. Sorted horizontal bars;
  a line for change over time.
- **Chart chrome:** heavy gridlines, a border, a shadow, a fill under everything, a legend where direct labels fit.
- **A chart with no period or source.** The title says what, when and from where.
- **A filter wired to one chart of four,** or filters nobody asked for. One selection updates everything it could
  apply to, or it isn't there.
- **Labels clipped at the axis; a chart spilling out of its card.** Open each chart with the largest and longest
  values before done.
- **Sample data shown as real.** Ask for the data, or mark it "Sample data" on screen.
- **Thousands of rows put straight into the page.** Paginate or virtualise past a few hundred.
- **Rows of identical dots or icons standing in for a quantity.** Print the number.
