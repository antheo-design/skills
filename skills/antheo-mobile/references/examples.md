# Examples: what goes wrong, and the fix

Each pair is shown live, before and after, on the Antheo skills page. Without the skill, then with it:

| Without | With |
|---|---|
| A field under 16px: the phone zooms in when you tap it | 16px fields: no zoom |
| The title runs under the status bar and the Dynamic Island | The page starts below them, in the safe area |
| The keyboard covers Save | Save rides up above the keyboard |

## When the default is wrong

A tool used at a desk on a tablet with a keyboard and trackpad, or a kiosk, takes its sizes from the pointer (coarse or fine), not from the width. A site that is mostly reading keeps the web's habits: no bottom bars, no sheets.
