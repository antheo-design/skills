# What generated copy gets wrong

Part of antheo-writing. Read it before calling the work done, and check the build against each habit.

The phrasings readers now take as machine-written (Wikipedia's field guide, detector catalogues, 2025 to 26).
`copy-lint.mjs` flags the first, sixth and seventh; with `--marketing` it also counts the devices of a selling page
(two-fragment headings, contrast frames, virtue words, strips of figures), whose budgets are in `marketing.md`.

- **Forced contrast:** "Not a tool. A platform.", "It's not just X, it's Y", "No X. No Y. Just Z." State the claim.
- **Two-beat slogans:** "Build faster. Ship smarter.", "Scale without limits." Say who it is for and what changes.
- **Two-noun feature names** ("Seamless integration", "Smart insights") and a list of three in every line. A claim;
  the one specific item.
- **A dash in nearly every sentence;** "serves as", "boasts", "offers" where "is" or "has" would do. A full stop or
  a comma; the plain verb.
- **A sub-line that repeats its heading** in other words, or one fact printed in four sections. Say it once.
- **Stock sample data:** Sarah Johnson, Jane Smith, Michael Chen, Acme, Globex, TechCorp; 47%, 3×, 10,000+, 99.9%.
  Names that fit this product's audience; real figures or none.
- **Leftovers:** "TODO", "Coming soon", "[Your company]", a template's old page title, "Certainly!", a comment
  describing obvious UI. Search for them before done.
- **Nothing specific:** a headline, sub-line and button with no number, name or date between them. One real detail.
