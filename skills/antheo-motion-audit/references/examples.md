# Examples: what goes wrong, and the fix

Each pair is shown live, before and after, on the Antheo skills page. Without the skill, then with it:

| Without | With |
|---|---|
| 11 durations, each picked on the day | Six, one per job: close, hover, small change, open, sheet, page |
| Eight different curves | One curve for arriving, one for leaving |
| A 600ms animation on a menu used all day | Removed: the more often it’s seen, the less it moves |

## When the default is wrong

A project with its own motion tokens is judged against those, not Antheo's. A game, a toy or a page whose motion is the content is out of scope for "remove": audit only what blocks input, ignores reduced motion or drops frames.
