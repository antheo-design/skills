# Antheo core, mini

The smallest form of the Antheo design skills, for a small or local model. Paste the style block as the first CSS,
follow the rules, and check the page at 390 and 1280px, light and dark. The full rules are in ANTHEO-CORE.md and the
skills: https://github.com/antheo-design/skills

## What always holds, and the four looks

**Always, in any style:** the person's task is clear and can be finished; every control works and every state is
reachable; keyboard, names, contrast, targets and zoom hold (**antheo-access**); real content survives every width and
both themes; the project's existing system, components and contracts are kept; nothing is invented; and every claim
comes with the check that shows it, with what wasn't checked said as unchecked.

**The look** is the style block and the rules below. **Quiet** is the default, for any new product with no system of
its own. **Studio** (a dense pro tool), **Editorial** (pages people read) and **Playful** (a product meant to be
enjoyed) are chosen only when the request asks for one or clearly is one; never ask, never switch for variety
(`references/looks.md`: the cues, what each changes, a finished page each). Every look keeps every rule, but for the
one exception it names (Studio: a hairline between panes; Editorial: serif headings). The look
gives way to the person's explicit direction or a reference they praise (**antheo-direction**) and to the project's
own tokens (**antheo-system**); then its numbers are choices to judge on their own terms, checked with the gate's
`--preset none`, which keeps every universal check.

Every number below is a **house default** unless it says otherwise; what never bends, what is guidance and what is
an experiment: `references/rules.md`.

**Parts and objects.** The look is for **parts**: fields, menus, lists, tables, tabs. An **object** (a bank card, a
dial, a radio tuner, a ticket) is drawn as the thing, with its own material inside its edge (antheo-surface
`references/objects.md`); a grey box with a label where the thing should be is its commonest failure.

## The rules (first sentences; ANTHEO-CORE.md has them whole)

Each with its limits; `references/rules.md` has each one whole, with its edge cases. Read it once before a screen.
1. **Few sizes.** At most four text sizes on an app screen (meta, body, title, page); a data screen may add `--t-num` for its key figures; a marketing section at most five (adds num and display).
2. **Two weights.** `--w-text` for text and prices, `--w-strong` for titles, labels and buttons.
3. **Headlines sit tight.** A marketing headline is `--t-display`, one colour, nine words and two lines at most; an app page title `--t-page`; card, plan and section titles `--t-title`.
4. **Cards have no outline.** `.card`: the surface, a faint edge, a soft drop. Never a visible or coloured border, never a card in a card.
5. **Almost no lines.** A hairline only between repeating rows of a list or table (in Studio, also between panes); a sticky bar takes one only once content scrolls under it.
6. **Three corners.** `--r-card` for cards, dialogs, menus, tables; `--r-control` for buttons, fields, chips; `--r-tag` for tags and the logo tile.
7. **Tags are rare, so they count.** One per card, two per screen.
8. **Colour means "on" or "look here".** `--accent` only on what is on or chosen and the highlight tags: four accent things a screen at most, one hue.
9. **One primary action.** One `.btn-primary`, after what it acts on, always enabled (while sending it keeps focus with `aria-disabled="true"` and ignores a second press). A recommended plan: its filled button, a `.tag-accent` and `--sh-lift`, at its neighbours' size.
10. **Fields are wells.** `.field`, its label above (`--t-body`, `--w-strong`, 6px apart); a helper line only for a constraint or a consequence (`--t-meta`, `--text-2`, 4px under); an error is the red inner edge and a message that names the fix (antheo-writing).
11. **Chrome only as named.** A section or a component gets no header, footer or navigation.
12. **Only what was asked.** No invented data, features, panels or copy; a sub-line only if it adds something, 12 words at most; the same feature count in every plan.
13. **Compact, with rhythm.** `--h-control`, `--h-row`, `--pad-card`, `--gap-section`; more space above a title than below.
14. **Widths.** A form `--w-form`, settings `--w-settings`, a pricing row `--w-pricing`, data `--w-data`, centred.
15. **One edge, matching siblings.** One left edge in the column for what is read; buttons go where the eye ends (a stacked form's on the fields' left edge; a card's, a wide dialog's or a switch list's at the end, main one last); the bar on the window gutter; cards in a row match in size, padding, order and button side; controls in a row share a height; a grid has one gap both ways.
16. **Two text tones.** `--text` for values, titles and labels; `--text-2` for descriptions, meta and placeholders; `--text-3` only for icons and ticks.
17. **A unit stays with its number:** on its baseline, 6px after it, `--t-body` `--text-2`, one line.
18. **Every state on the same layout,** one made-up person throughout, no placeholder text.
19. **Icons are few and grey:** 16px, 1.5 stroke, `--text-3`, for navigation, a named icon-only button and list ticks. No decorative icon in a primary button; a spinner and a done check are states (antheo-components).
20. **Count before done.** In the 1280px screenshots, light and dark: text sizes, weights, corners, accent things, tags, hairlines, each within these limits, and the main task on the first screen.
21. **Arm the gate, and pass it.** At the start, `gate.mjs arm <page> --copy <source>` (`--preset studio`, `editorial` or `playful` for a look, `none` for the project's own system, `--landing` for a landing page, `--ref` for references); before done, `gate.mjs check` says PASS, or DONE, UNRESOLVED told to the person (antheo-review `references/gate.md`). When exploring a throwaway sketch or early direction, skip the gate until the person picks one to keep, then arm it before calling the build done. A PASS is the floor.
