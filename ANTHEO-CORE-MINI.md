# Antheo core, mini

The smallest form of the Antheo design skills, for a small or local model. Paste the style block as the first CSS,
follow the rules, and check the page at 390 and 1280px, light and dark. The full rules are in ANTHEO-CORE.md and the
skills: https://github.com/antheo-design/skills

## What always holds, and the Quiet preset

**Always, in any style:** the person's task is clear and can be finished; every control works and every state is
reachable; keyboard, names, contrast, targets and zoom hold (**antheo-access**); real content survives every width and
both themes; the project's existing system, components and contracts are kept; nothing is invented; and every claim
comes with the check that shows it, with what wasn't checked said as unchecked.

**The Quiet preset** is the style block and the look in the rules below (rules 1 to 8, 13 to 17 and 19, the plan, field and logo-tile looks in rules 9 to 11, and the counts in rule 20): the default look for a new product
with no system of its own. It gives way, in this order, to the person's explicit direction ("bold", "editorial", a
reference they praise: **antheo-direction**) and to the project's own tokens and components (**antheo-system**).
Then its numbers stop applying: outlined cards, pill buttons, more weights, larger or serif type and several meaningful
colours are choices to judge on their own terms, not defects. Keep the restraint either way, and every device earns
its place. Check such work with the gate's `--preset none`, which keeps every universal check and drops the Quiet ones.

## The rules (first sentences; ANTHEO-CORE.md has them whole)

1. **Few sizes.** An app screen uses at most four text sizes (meta, body, title, page); a data screen may add `--t-num` for its key figures and nothing else; a marketing section at most five (adds num and display).
2. **Two weights.** `--w-text` for text and prices, `--w-strong` for titles, labels and buttons.
3. **Headlines sit tight.** A marketing headline is `--t-display`, one colour, nine words at most, two lines at most.
4. **Cards have no outline.** A card is `.card`: the surface on the page, a faint edge and a soft drop. Never a visible border, never a coloured edge, never a card inside a card.
5. **Almost no lines.** A hairline (`--line`) only between repeating rows of a list or table.
6. **Three corners.** Cards, dialogs, menus and tables `--r-card`; buttons, fields and chips `--r-control`; tags and the logo tile `--r-tag`.
7. **Tags are rare, so they count.** At most one per card and two per screen.
8. **Colour means "on" or "look here".** `--accent` only on a switch that is on, a checked box, the chosen chip and the highlight tags of rule 7: at most four accent things on a screen (a list of switches counts as one), all in the one accent hue.
9. **One primary action.** The only `.btn-primary`, after what it acts on, always enabled (while it is sending it stays focusable with `aria-disabled="true"` and ignores a second press). A recommended plan is its filled button, a `.tag-accent` "Recommended" and `--sh-lift`, at exactly its neighbours' size.
10. **Fields are wells.** `.field`, with its label above (`--t-body`, `--w-strong`, `--text`), 6px apart. A helper line only for a constraint or a consequence ("JPG or PNG, up to 2 MB"; "At least 8 characters"), in `--t-meta` `--text-2`, 4px under. An error is the red inner edge and a short message under the field naming the fix (antheo-writing).
11. **Chrome only as named.** A "section", a "component" or a page that shows components gets no site header, footer or navigation.
12. **Only what was asked.** No invented data, features, panels or copy.
13. **Compact, with rhythm.** Controls `--h-control`, rows `--h-row`, card padding `--pad-card`, sections `--gap-section` apart; more space above a title than below it; equal padding on all sides of a card or bar.
14. **Widths.** A form `--w-form`; settings one `--w-settings` column; a pricing row `--w-pricing`; data `--w-data`.
15. **One edge, matching siblings.** Everything in the column shares its left edge; the bar's contents sit on the window gutter.
16. **Two text tones in a card.** `--text` for values, titles and labels; `--text-2` for descriptions and meta.
17. **A unit stays with its number:** on its baseline, 6px after it, `--t-body` `--text-2`, on one line.
18. **Every state on the same layout,** with one made-up person throughout and no placeholder text.
19. **Icons are few and grey.** 16px, 1.5 stroke, `--text-3`: navigation, an icon-only button (with a name), a tick in a list. No decorative icon inside a primary button; a spinner while it works and a check when it's done are states, not decoration (antheo-components).
20. **Count before done.** Open the 1280px screenshots, light and dark, and count: text sizes, weights, corner sizes, accent things, tags, hairlines.
21. **Arm the gate, and pass it.** At the start, arm antheo-review's gate on every page the work touches (`gate.mjs arm <url-or-file> --copy <source folder>`, plus `--preset none` when the project has its own system); before saying done, `gate.mjs check` must say PASS, or after three rounds DONE, UNRESOLVED with what is left, told to the person. How much to check beyond the gate depends on the size of the change (antheo-review, "How much to check"). Setup for each agent: antheo-review `references/gate.md`.
